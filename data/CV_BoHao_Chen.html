Research Interest

My research centers on natural language processing for Chinese and Korean. Coming from a sociology background, I aim to model the identity, discourse, and cultural nuance embedded in text. I am especially interested in cross-lingual and low-resource NLP, since Chinese and Korean pose distinct challenges for English-centric models.
